Privacy Policy for TrainCore

Effective date: February 26, 2026

TrainCore is provided as a free application. This app is designed to function primarily using on-device storage.

Information We Collect

TrainCore does not collect, store, or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

All workout-related data is stored locally on your device, including:

  • Workout notes
  • Personal records
  • Workout sessions
  • Progress and completion status
  • App settings (such as language, theme, and timer preferences)

This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to external servers.

Third-Party Services

TrainCore does not use third-party analytics, advertising services, or tracking SDKs.

The app does not display advertisements.

How Information Is Used

All stored data is used solely to:

  • Provide the app’s core functionality
  • Save your workout history and preferences locally
  • Maintain app performance and stability

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you maintain control over your information. We do not access, transmit, or share your data.

Children’s Privacy

TrainCore does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
